_13 nov 2026. The twelve people of `08_session_review.md`, run again through the real chain after phase D (the composer, weekly targets, the trigger swap, the fill and the coach's eye). The marathon and "keep it going" get a real plan. What a coach would still change sits inside the program files (dose spread across the week, unloaded variants for intermediates, a few effort lines), not in how the week is built.
