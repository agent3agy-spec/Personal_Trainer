# Sending the trainer to a friend

Send the kettlebell card (card.png, or card-fr.png in French), then this message with the link.

## English
💪 I made you a personal trainer. Tap the link, answer a few questions (about 3 minutes) and it builds your plan, then a one-page sheet for every session. Free, and everything stays on your phone.
👉 https://personal-trainer-ear.pages.dev

## Français
💪 Je t'ai fait un coach perso. Touche le lien, réponds à quelques questions (3 minutes environ) et il te construit ton plan, puis une fiche d'une page pour chaque séance. Gratuit, et tout reste sur ton téléphone.
👉 https://personal-trainer-ear.pages.dev

## If they ask "how do I use it"
1. Tap the link, then Start.
2. Answer the questions. Not sure? Write it in your own words.
3. Before each workout, open the link again and tap "Today's session". After it, tell it how it went.
Tip: pin the chat or save the link, it is the same link every time.
